Partline pump-flow troubleshooting knowledge, revision 1.
This is an illustrative service note for the Partline demo, not an OEM manual or a confirmed diagnosis. It applies to a two-group espresso machine when the pump is unusually loud and no water comes out of either group head.

The shared water supply feeds the inlet hose (Partline component ID: inlet-hose). A low water level, a closed external supply valve, or a kink or restriction visible in the inlet line can starve the pump. A starved pump may sound louder even if the pump itself works. Check the tank level or the visible external water supply first. Stop further brewing attempts if there is no supply.

The water pump (Partline component ID: water-pump) pressurizes feed water for the brew groups. If the supply is available and neither group flows, a qualified technician can check for air ingress, an inlet restriction, or a pump fault. Pump noise alone does not prove the pump needs replacement.

The brew solenoid valve (Partline component ID: solenoid-valve) routes brew water and drainage. A sticking or restricted valve is more plausible if a single group is affected or water goes into the drip tray during brewing. When both groups stop simultaneously, investigate their shared water path before suspecting an individual brew valve.

Useful operator observations are the tank level or status of the visible external supply, whether the sound comes from the pump, and whether one or both brew groups are affected. The user should not open the machine, bypass safety devices, operate it dry, or order a replacement based on symptoms alone. Have a qualified technician confirm the failing component before replacement.
